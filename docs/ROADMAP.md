# Roadmap

Version 1.0 has four models (metallurgic, hydraulic, acoustic, cosmic), each staging capture, the war machine, and warding off as one material’s physics, with lines of flight between them and a Sources panel that separates the book, its sources, the physics, and the divergences. What follows is planned or under consideration, roughly in order.

## Polarization: the town

The town is the fourth of the book’s five processes, a circuit “defined by entries and exits” (432) that polarizes a milieu rather than stratifying a territory. Each model’s Sources panel names a lead. In the liquid world, sinks inside the flow as well as sources. In the acoustic world, frequency rather than resonance, melodic lines against the State’s harmonic cross sections (433-34). In the gravity world, centers that let flows pass through rather than hold them.

## Encompassment

Ecumenical organizations pass between formations rather than belonging to one (435), so a single body of matter cannot stage them. One option is a view that sets several models side by side, with what passes between them drawn as flows. Another is a model with more than one center of each kind.

## Accessibility

- Text alternatives that describe the current state of a simulation, not only its name.
- Colors checked for readers with color blindness, and keyboard access to every control.
- A reduced-motion mode that shows a still frame alongside the narrator’s chart (the still frame exists already).

## Archiving and citation

- A DOI for each release, by connecting the repository to Zenodo.
- Stable links to particular configurations for citation (the address bar already records every setting).

## Maintenance

- Keep each model’s balance measured after any change to its physics (`npm run sim`), and recheck quotations (`npm run check:atp`).
- The calibration of the metallurgic model’s nucleation threshold is recorded in [notes/nucleation-calibration.md](notes/nucleation-calibration.md).
