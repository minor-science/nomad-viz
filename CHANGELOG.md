# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-18)

The first public release. All four models were rebuilt, starting from their physics.

- **Metallurgic:** a Metropolis lattice gas with grain orientations and a finite reservoir, with stocks that nucleate past the threshold of classical nucleation theory, clans that block and wear them, and smiths that carve along grain boundaries. Lines of flight: smiths walled in by the stock, clans seeding stocks, clans turning smith and smiths settling as clans.
- **Hydraulic:** a heated liquid layer with a through-flow (stable fluids with buoyancy), read as laminar layers, convection cells, and turbulence, with dye from fixed sources, raids, and crossings between regimes. Labor, the corvée, and the journeymen give its social reading.
- **Acoustic:** metronomes on a shared board as phase oscillators, with one bell for all, villages keeping their own hours, contagion from neighbor to neighbor, pacemakers, and raids.
- **Cosmic:** Newtonian gravity around a capital city, with societies stripped by tides, companies caught by dynamical friction or raiding the capital city and riding on across the field, and societies hardening into small States or throwing off companies.
- **Sources** for every model in four parts: what the book says, what it borrows, the physics, and where they part ways, with every quotation checked against the book.
- **A new interface:** plain text, one narrator, labels that follow features, a legend, full screen, and layouts for phones (including a turned channel for the hydraulic model).
- **Tools:** a headless runner for measuring models (`npm run sim`) and a quotation checker that covers the documentation.

## 0.1.0 (2026-04)
